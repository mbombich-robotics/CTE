Unit 5 — Week 24

Artificial
Intelligence

How machines learn — and why it matters

You've programmed L.E.O. to sense and respond. Now take one step further: what if the robot learned those responses from examples instead of rules you wrote? This unit covers how machine learning works, what it means to train a model, and the ethical responsibilities that come with putting AI into the world.

Lessons & Activities

Lesson 6.1
What Is AI?
Artificial intelligence vs. machine learning vs. deep learning. Three types of ML, how a model learns from data, neural networks, and bias in training data.

Lesson 6.2
Ethics in AI
Real cases of biased AI. The engineering canons applied to autonomous decisions. Who is responsible when AI causes harm — and what responsible engineers actually do.

Activity 6.3
Teachable Machine
Train your own image classifier in the browser using Google's Teachable Machine. No code required — just examples. Then deliberately break it to see how training data shapes what a model knows.
