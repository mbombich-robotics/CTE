Direct Current (DC)

Electrons flow continuously in one single direction. Voltage remains constant over time.

  • Sources: Batteries, USB ports, solar panels
  • Used by: Microcontrollers, LEDs, computers
Reference Points

What is Earth Ground?

The Planet as a Conductor

"Ground" refers to the literal earth, which acts as a massive conductor held at 0 Volts baseline.

  • Houses utilize metal ground rods driven 8+ feet into soil.
  • Ties system reference to a stable 0V baseline.

Safety Function

If a live wire touches a metal appliance casing, fault current flows directly to earth ground via green safety ground wire, tripping the breaker instead of flowing through a person.

Core Equation

Ohm's Law: V = I × R

V = I × R

Voltage (Volts) = Current (Amps) × Resistance (Ohms)

I = V / R — Current increases with Voltage, decreases with Resistance

R = V / I — Resistance equals Voltage divided by Current flow

Body Path Mechanics

Why Current Path Matters

⚠️ Hand-to-Hand Path

CRITICAL RISK

Current enters one arm, travels straight across the chest cavity, and exits through the other arm. Because the current vector passes directly through the heart muscle, it poses the highest risk of triggering ventricular fibrillation.

💼 The "One Hand in Pocket" Rule:
High-voltage technicians working on live gear keep one hand tucked inside a pocket. This breaks the hand-to-hand loop, ensuring any accidental contact path goes to ground through the legs rather than across the heart.

⚠️ EXTREME HAZARD: Current flows up one leg and down the other!

⚡ Why Step Potential Happens

When thousands of volts discharge into the earth, voltage drops exponentially with distance from the strike point. If your feet touch two points at different voltage levels, your legs complete a high-voltage circuit.

🦘 Practical Survival Rule:
If caught near a downed power line or strike zone: keep feet together and shuffle or bunny-hop away. With zero distance between your feet, the potential difference drops to near 0 Volts!
