Part D · Predict It

No calculator needed. Answer each one and give a one-sentence reason.

  1. The voltage stays the same and the resistance doubles. What happens to the current?
  2. The resistance stays the same and the voltage triples. What happens to the current?
  3. A wire connects + straight to − (about 0 Ω). What happens to the current, and why is it dangerous?
  4. The switch in a circuit is open. What is the current, and what is the resistance of the open gap?
