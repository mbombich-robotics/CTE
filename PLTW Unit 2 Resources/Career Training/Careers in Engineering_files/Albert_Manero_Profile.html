Meet a Solutionary

Albert Manero

Mechanical Engineer Limbitless Solutions PLTW Alumni
His Story

As a high school student in PLTW, Albert Manero discovered 3D printing in his engineering class. He was captivated by what the technology could do — but he didn't know yet what problem he'd use it to solve.

That changed when he learned that prosthetic limbs for children cost tens of thousands of dollars — far beyond what most families could afford. Children who needed bionic arms were simply going without.

Albert saw a problem that engineering could fix. He founded Limbitless Solutions, a nonprofit that designs and 3D prints free bionic arms for children in need. The arms are custom-fitted, functional — and they often look like something out of a superhero movie.

Limbitless has provided arms to hundreds of children, partnered with major studios (including Marvel and EA), and grown into a full research organization at the University of Central Florida. It started with one PLTW student and an idea.
