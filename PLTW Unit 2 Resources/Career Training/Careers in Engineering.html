Careers in Engineering

Engineering is a field of immense scope within the U.S. Federal Government Standard Occupational Classification, which recognizes 17 engineering specialties. 

  • Aerospace
  • Agricultural
  • Biomedical (Bioengineering)
  • Chemical
  • Civil
  • Computer Hardware
  • Electrical
  • Electronics
  • Environmental
  • Health and Safety
  • Industrial
  • Marine (Naval Architect)
  • Materials
  • Mechanical
  • Mining (Geologic)
  • Nuclear
  • Petroleum
  • Engineers, All Other

This is just the tip of the iceberg! Many other engineering disciplines, such as plastics engineering, systems engineering, software engineering, and entertainment engineering, are recognized by various professional societies.

Making an Impact

As an engineer, you use math and science—with a dose of creativity, persistence, and design thinking—to solve practical problems. Keep reading to learn more about tasks you can do in each engineering discipline. 

Aerospace Engineering

  • Make planes safer and more fuel efficient
  • Design remotely piloted aircraft and rotorcraft
  • Investigate accidents
  • Design or build instrumentation to help colonize space

Electrical Engineering

  • Design an effective radar system
  • Determine new ways to harness electrical power
  • Test aircraft electrical equipment
  • Oversee electric utilities
Materials Engineering
  • Improve the safety of combat uniforms
  • Develop material to replace burn victims’ skin
  • Design protective materials to be used for space travel
  • Develop materials that efficiently conduct electricity
Agricultural Engineering
  • Develop farming methods that emphasize sustainability
  • Design remote sensing equipment to study plant life
  • Provide advice about water quality and conservation issues
  • Improve food safety
Electronics Engineering
  • Create a cell phone design
  • Build video games
  • Invent medical microrobots that can examine the human body
Mechanical Engineering
  • Design cars
  • Determine new uses for lasers
  • Maintain power-producing equipment, such as wind turbines
  • Build production machines
Biomedical Engineering
  • Design artificial organs, limbs, or joints
  • Manufacture medications for diseases such as cancer or AIDS
  • Determine ways to prevent sports injuries
  • Work alongside medical professionals to refine medical procedures
Environmental Engineering
  • Clean up hazardous waste sites
  • Design technology for reprocessing materials
  • Design methods for reducing pollutants in the atmosphere
  • Ensure adherence to local regulations
  • Develop solutions to recycling and waste disposal problems
Mining (Geologic) Engineering
  • Locate gold or silver deposits
  • Consult on mining permit and compliance issues
  • Create safety procedures for the mining industry
  • Design efficient ore extraction equipment and methods
Chemical Engineering
  • Create more efficient fuel sources
  • Improve food processing techniques
  • Design innovative gene therapy tools
  • Devise a large-scale vaccine production process
Health and Safety Engineering
  • Prevent workplace accidents
  • Enforce measures to prevent nuclear accidents
  • Reduce traffic accidents
  • Analyze new equipment to ensure it can be operated safely
  • Educate the workforce on the proper use of safety gear
Nuclear Engineering
  • Inspect or maintain nuclear submarines
  • Identify new medical uses for radiation
  • Develop methods to utilize nuclear energy for space travel
Civil Engineering
  • Identify weather safety designs for buildings
  • Build bridges to direct traffic over obstacles
  • Develop vertical farm systems for urban rooftops or balconies
  • Design airport runways, taxiways, and aprons
Industrial Engineering
  • Improve efficiency in hospitals
  • Determine machine layout for manufacturing facilities
  • Design stadium facilities for convenient use
  • Determine how large a warehouse must be
Petroleum Engineering
  • Design new methods for extracting oil or natural gas from the Earth
  • Determine the most cost-effective extraction locations
  • Identify locations containing oil and natural gas
Computer Hardware Engineering
  • Design a computer circuit board, processor, or memory card
  • Develop hardware for a smart device
  • Build computer systems for national companies
  • Analyze system data to reconfigure hardware

Marine and Ocean Engineering
  • Build or maintain oil rigs
  • Build robots for deep-sea exploration
  • Design marine or naval vessels
  • Improve submarine technology

Engineering, All Others
  • Write technical specifications and reports
  • Sell equipment and services to engineering firms 
  • Help an end-user customer troubleshoot a product
  • Design special effects for movies
  • Design roller coasters and playground equipment
  • Improve our world!
