SAFE OPERATING PROCEDURE

CORDLESS DRILL / DRIVER

⚠ WARNING Do NOT use this tool until Mr. Bombich has trained you, signed you off, and given you permission today.
🥽SAFETY GLASSES
required
👟CLOSED-TOE SHOES
required
💇LONG HAIR TIED BACK
sleeves rolled up
🧤NO GLOVES
they wrap on the bit
💍NO JEWELRY
or hoodie strings

1 · BEFORE YOU START — setup checks

  • I have permission, I know what I'm drilling or driving, and the hole is marked.
  • Drill and battery are in good shape: no cracks, battery clicks in firmly. Broken? Don't use it. Report it.
  • Trigger LOCKED (direction switch in the center) before touching the chuck or changing bits.
  • Bit or driver tip is sharp, the right type and size, seated fully and tightened in the chuck.
  • Direction set (forward to drill or drive), speed set (1 = driving / large bits, 2 = small holes), and clutch set.
  • Work is clamped or held in a vise, never in your hand or on your lap.
  • I checked what's behind and under the work: the bench, wires, my hand, my leg.

2 · WHILE DRILLING — operating procedures

  • Firm grip, balanced stance, drill square to the work. Use both hands.
  • Keep your free hand away from the bit, and never behind the spot where the bit will come through.
  • Start slow until the bit bites, then use steady pressure. Let the bit cut and don't force it.
  • If the bit binds or the drill twists, release the trigger right away. Don't fight it.
  • Ease off as the bit breaks through. Back out often to clear chips on deep holes.
  • Drill in FORWARD all the way through and then out. Use reverse only to back out a jammed bit.
  • Bits get HOT. Don't touch the bit or chips right after drilling.
  • Finger off the trigger when you're not drilling. Never carry it with your finger on the trigger.

3 · WHEN YOU'RE DONE — clean-up

  • Lock the trigger and remove the battery.
  • Remove the bit (careful, it may be hot) and return it to the drill index.
  • Put the battery on the charger and the drill back in its spot.
  • Unclamp the part, deburr sharp edges, and brush chips off the bench. Never use your hands.
  • Report dull or broken bits, damaged batteries, or a drill that isn't working right to Mr. Bombich.
LEAVE IT SAFE: battery charging  ·  chuck empty  ·  drill put away  ·  bench clean  ·  ready for the next person
🛑 Injury, near-miss, or something wrong? STOP and tell Mr. Bombich.
