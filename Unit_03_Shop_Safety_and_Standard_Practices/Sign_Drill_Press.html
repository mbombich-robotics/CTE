1 · BEFORE YOU START — setup checks

  • I have permission, I know what I'm drilling, and the hole is marked and center-punched.
  • Area is clear: no slip/trip hazards, table clear of tools, one operator at the machine.
  • Machine is in good shape: pulley cover closed, no lock/tag. Broken? Don't use it. Report it.
  • Bit is sharp and the right size, tight in the chuck, and the CHUCK KEY IS REMOVED.
  • Work is clamped in a vise or to the table, never hand-held.
  • Depth stop is set and table height checked, so the bit won't hit the table or vise.
  • I know where the ON/OFF switch and E-stop are and can reach them without reaching past the bit.

2 · WHILE DRILLING — operating procedures

  • Start the machine and check that the bit runs true. If it wobbles, shut it off and fix it.
  • Keep hands at least 3 inches from the bit. Never reach around, over, or under a spinning bit.
  • Feed with steady, light pressure. Let the bit cut and don't force it.
  • Peck drill: back out often to clear chips and cool the bit. Use cutting fluid on metal.
  • Ease off as the bit breaks through the bottom. That's when it grabs.
  • If the work catches or spins, let go, step back, and hit OFF. Never grab spinning work.
  • Stop the machine completely before adjusting or clearing chips. Never leave it running.

3 · WHEN YOU'RE DONE — clean-up

  • Switch OFF and wait for the spindle to stop on its own. Never slow the chuck with your hand.
  • Unclamp the part and deburr sharp edges. Hot chips and metal parts can burn.
  • Remove the bit and return it to the drill index. Put the chuck key, vise, and clamps away.
  • Brush chips off the table, base, and floor. Never use your hands or compressed air.
  • Report dull or broken bits and any damage to Mr. Bombich.
LEAVE IT SAFE: power OFF  ·  chuck empty  ·  key in holder  ·  table clean  ·  floor swept  ·  ready for the next person
