Vicksburg Robotics Lab

Critical Lab Safety Rules

  • Mandatory PPE: Wear safety glasses, closed-toe shoes, and proper gloves when handling chemicals or raw stock.
  • Entanglement Prevention: Secure long hair back, tuck loose clothing, and remove lanyard/jewelry before approaching machinery.
  • Hazard Reporting: Report damaged tool guards, frayed cords, or liquid spills to instructors immediately.
  • Zero Horseplay: No running, throwing objects, or disruptive behavior in the shop area.
Tool Authorization

Tool & Equipment Operation

Operational Rules

  • Training First: Never operate any machine without prior training and explicit instructor authorization.
  • Pre-Flight Inspection: Inspect tool bodies, blades, guards, and cables before powering on.
  • Clean & Return: Clean debris and return tools to designated rack spaces after use.
⛔ NEVER Operate Equipment With:
• Missing or bypassed safety guards
• Damaged or dull cutting blades
• Exposed electrical wiring or frayed cords
• Active Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) locks attached
Lab Culture

Respect the Workspace

Organization Standards

  • Clutter-Free Zone: Keep walkways and machine tables clear of debris.
  • Battery Management: Recharge power tool batteries immediately after lab sessions.
  • Material Storage: Store raw stock flat on floor racks to prevent tipping.

Peer Respect

  • Project Integrity: Never touch or move another team's project work.
  • Secured Machinery: Never leave running or un-secured tools unattended.
  • Food/Drink Policy: No food or beverages allowed in machining zones.
Facility Control

Lab Access & Facility Security

  • Authorized Hours: Access the robotics lab only during scheduled class hours or under supervisor presence.
  • Visitor Control: Do not admit unauthorized individuals or guests into the shop without instructor clearance.
  • Facility Lock-Up: Verify machine power drops and door locks are secured when leaving the lab.
  • Incident Reporting: Report unauthorized entry or improper tool usage immediately.
Emergency Readiness

Emergency Response Protocols

🧯 Fire Extinguishers

Know exact locations of ABC extinguishers and emergency exit pathways.

🩹 First Aid Kits

Locate eyewash stations and first aid kits before starting work.

🚨 Injury Reporting

Report all injuries—no matter how minor—to instructors immediately.

Federal Standards

OSHA Overview

What is OSHA?

Occupational Safety & Health Administration: Ensures safe and healthful working conditions by setting and enforcing standards across private sector employers and educational labs.

3 Key Functions

  • Standard-Setting: Machine guarding, PPE, hazard communication
  • Inspections & Enforcement: On-site compliance audits
  • Training & Outreach: Worker safety education
Legal Rights

You Have Rights at Work

Core Worker Rights

  • Work in a safe environment free from recognized hazards
  • Receive safety training in a language you understand
  • Ask safety questions without fear of retaliation
  • Use required safety equipment provided by employer

Protection Rights

  • File confidential safety complaints directly with OSHA
  • Refuse unsafe work assignments that pose imminent danger
Employer Obligations

Employer Responsibilities

  • Hazard-Free Workplace: Provide conditions meeting all OSHA safety standards.
  • Free Safety Gear: Employers must provide and pay for required PPE (goggles, hard hats, respirators).
  • Comprehensive Training: Train workers on tool hazards and emergency response.
  • Injury Response Protocols: Clear procedures for reporting and treating workplace injuries.
29 CFR Code Standards

Key Standards for Our Lab

Machine Guarding

29 CFR 1910.212

Requires physical barriers on all rotating shafts, belts, gears, and blades.

Walking Surfaces

29 CFR 1910.22

Keep floors clean, dry, and clear of cords or tripping hazards.

Hazard Communication

29 CFR 1910.1200

Mandatory container labels and Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for chemicals.

Energy Isolation

Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Fundamentals

Definition

Securing all hazardous energy sources (electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, mechanical) with physical locks and warning tags before servicing equipment.

Student LOTO Rule

  • Students do NOT attach or remove LOTO locks.
  • If a machine has a lock/tag attached, DO NOT OPERATE IT under any circumstance.

Safety Gear

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

🥽

Safety Glasses

ANSI Z87.1 approved; required at all times in shop.

👟

Closed-Toe Shoes

Leather or sturdy canvas; no sandals or mesh shoes.

🧤

Chemical Gloves

Handling solvents/chemicals only. NEVER near rotating tools!

⚠️ Rotating Equipment Hazard: Remove gloves, tie back long hair, and remove jewelry before operating drill presses, lathes, or mills!

What's in an SDS?

  • Section 1-3: Chemical identity & hazard identification
  • Section 4-6: First aid & accidental release measures

Hazard Identification

What Are the Hazards in Our Shop?

General Environment

  • Slips, trips, falls
  • Floor clutter & cords
  • Excessive noise levels

Tool Specific

  • Rotating spindles
  • Sharp material edges
  • Mechanical pinch points

Chemical

  • Solvents & degreasers
  • Cyanoacrylate glues
  • Cutting fluids & dust
