Unit 3 — Safety First

Shop Safety &
Standard Practices

Know the rules. Respect the tools. Go home in one piece.

Before you touch a machine, you need to understand why the rules exist. This unit covers OSHA standards, hazard recognition, Lockout/Tagout procedures, PPE requirements, and how real engineering failures happen when safety is ignored. The knowledge here protects you every day in the shop.
