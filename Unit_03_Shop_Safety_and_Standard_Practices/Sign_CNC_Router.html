1 · BEFORE YOU START — setup checks

  • Area is clear: no tools or scrap on the bed, no trip hazards, nothing in the gantry's path.
  • Machine is in good shape, with no lock or tag on it. Broken? Don't use it. Report it.
  • Bit is the right one, sharp, collet tight, and the wrenches REMOVED.
  • Material is screwed or clamped down, with clamps and screws outside the toolpath. Never tape alone.
  • Correct G-code is loaded and the simulation looks right.
  • X, Y, and Z zero are set, and the bit will clear the clamps when it travels.
  • I know where the E-STOP is, and my hand can reach it.

2 · WHILE CUTTING — operating procedures

  • NEVER leave the machine running unattended. No phones or headphones.
  • Hands OUT of the work area while the spindle is turning. Never clear chips by hand.
  • Run new G-code at 50% feed override first. Never go above 100% without approval.
  • Watch the first pass: Z-height correct and material not lifting or shifting.
  • HIT THE E-STOP for a broken or loose bit, moving material, smoke, grinding or crashing sounds, or anyone in the way.
  • After an E-stop: wait for the spindle to stop, then tell Mr. Bombich. Don't reset without approval.
  • Fire: E-stop, shout "FIRE!", and step away. Mr. Bombich handles the extinguisher.

3 · WHEN YOU'RE DONE — clean-up

  • Job complete, spindle fully stopped, and the machine idle (no active G-code).
  • Remove the part carefully, since edges may be sharp. Remove the screws and clamps.
  • Vacuum chips and dust from the bed, rails, and floor.
  • Remove the bit, wipe it clean, and return it to the tool holder. Log the job in the machine notebook.
  • Return the machine to home. Power down the controller if you're the last user.
LEAVE IT SAFE: spindle off  ·  machine homed  ·  bed vacuumed  ·  tools put away  ·  job logged  ·  ready for the next person
🛑 Injury, near-miss, or something wrong? STOP and tell Mr. Bombich.
