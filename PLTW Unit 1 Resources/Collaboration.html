Collaboration

You have probably worked in collaboration with others in formal and informal settings. Collaboration includes teamwork and incorporates other aspects, such as valuing different perspectives, treating everyone as equals, and efficiently sharing work responsibilities. 

TermDefinition
collaboration Collaboration is the action of two or more people working together through idea sharing to accomplish a common goal.

  • All professionals use collaboration strategies in day-to-day operations and project work.

  • Employers consistently seek employees who are effective collaborators. It is estimated that 60%–80% of an engineer's time is spent in technical collaboration.

Teamwork

Teams—groups of individuals working together—must work hard to develop productive working relationships despite personal differences and different cultural practices.

TermDefinition
teamA team is a collection of individuals, each with his or her own expertise, brought together to benefit a common goal.  
brainstormingA group technique for solving problems, generating ideas, stimulating creative thinking, etc., by unrestrained spontaneous participation in discussion.

Benefits of a Team

  • Shared workloads and responsibility.

  • Broader diversity in knowledge and skill.

  • More productive brainstorming.

  • Ability to accomplish more than if work was done independently.

  • Sense of belonging to a successful process.

  • Chances for leadership and personal satisfaction.

Positive Interdependence 

Often, team members are responsible for one part of a larger goal. Each team member contributes only their part with no responsibility to support other team members. But successful collaboration requires team members to experience positive interdependence. Positive interdependence means that each team member recognizes that their individual success depends on the success of their teammates. 

Every team member must make unique contributions to the work and support other team members in their work. Each team member must understand their own strengths and weaknesses. A successful team includes members who are both task oriented and process oriented.

Collaborative Problem-Solving

To find success, a group can organize their work in many ways. One is the collaborative problem-solving process outlined in the following steps. You can use this checklist in your classes when you are asked to work collaboratively. 

TermDefinition
team normsA set of rules or guidelines that a team establishes to shape the interaction of team members with one other.
consensusGeneral agreement.

IMPORTANT: Every design decision requires discussion and consensus from all team members. 

  • Listen to input from all team members.

  • Follow meeting schedules and project timelines.

  • Follow Conflict Resolution(opens in a new tab) protocols.

  • Don't be defensive with your teammates.

  • Support each other.

  • Be honest with each other about ideas and challenges.

  • Pay attention to what other team members need and help them do things for themselves.

  • Celebrate success and have fun!
