Journal Article, DOI Available

The digital object identifier (DOI) is a unique alphanumeric string that provides a persistent link to the content. If the DOI is listed with the reference information, include it in your citation as indicated.

Format

Author, A. A. (year). Title of article. Title of Periodical, volume number(issue number), p–p. https://doi.org/xxxxx

Examples

Wilder, S. (2015). Impact of problem-based learning on academic achievement in high school: A systematic review. Educational Review, 67(4). 414–435. http://dx.doi.org/10.1080/00131911.2014.974511

Allen, D. E., Donham, R. S., & Bernhardt, S. A. (2011). Problem-based learning. New Directions for Teaching and Learning, 128, 19. http://dx.doi.org/10.1002/tl.465
