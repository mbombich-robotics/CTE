Design • Build • Drive • Make

A semester-long, hands-on introduction to engineering and robotics. You'll follow the Engineering Design Process, learn Fusion 360 CAD, and build a real robot from scratch — then program it to drive.

You'll also explore mechanisms — gear trains, pulleys, and motion conversion — using parts you design and 3D print. By the end of the semester, you'll have a working robot and a portfolio of engineering projects.

What You'll Master

Apply the Engineering Design Process — define, sketch, prototype, test, iterate
Model robot components in Fusion 360 and assemble a full robot CAD model
Operate 3D printers and manage a print queue for real robot parts
Assemble the L.E.O. robot platform — frame, motors, wheels, electronics
Write MicroPython programs to drive the robot forward, turn, and stop
Build and analyze gear trains and pulley systems
Practice shop safety and earn tool certifications
Document your work and build a semester portfolio

Tools & Equipment

Fusion 360 CAD software
Raspberry Pi Pico 2W running MicroPython
BambuLab 3D printers with PLA filament
Hand drill, calipers, and precision measurement tools
Teacher-cut CNC robot decks and sensor mounts
Electronics lab — wiring, connectors, and motor drivers
