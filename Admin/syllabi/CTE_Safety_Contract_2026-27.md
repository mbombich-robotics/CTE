## Part 1 — Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

___ I will wear safety glasses whenever I am working with, or near, shop tools, machinery, or electronics — even if I am only observing.

___ I will wear gloves and hearing protection when required, as directed by the instructor.

___ I understand that PPE is never optional when required. There are no exceptions.

---

## Part 2 — Shop & Workspace Safety

___ Every piece of equipment in the lab has a laminated operation and safety sheet posted nearby. I will read it before using any machine for the first time.

___ I will receive awareness-level safety training for all machines in the lab — including the CNC router, laser cutter, and plasma cutter — even if I do not operate them directly. I will not touch or approach any machine without the instructor's permission.

___ I will return all tools to their designated location after use and leave my workspace clean before leaving class. This is the 5S standard.

___ I will not run, roughhouse, or behave unsafely in the lab at any time.

___ When I am unsure how to safely complete a task, I will stop and ask the instructor before proceeding.

---

## Part 3 — Emergency Procedures

___ There are five red emergency stop buttons located throughout the shop. These cut power to the machines and are for emergencies only.

___ If I press a stop button — for any reason, including by accident — I will immediately report it to the instructor. There is no penalty for an accidental press, but every press must be reported.

___ In any emergency, I will follow the instructor's directions immediately and without hesitation.

---

## Part 4 — Electronics & Device Use Policy

___ I will use only district-provided devices and my single district-issued account for all classwork. Personal devices are not authorized for class use.

___ I will not attempt to use additional accounts, share login credentials, or access another student's account.

___ I will not use a VPN or any tool or technique designed to circumvent district network security. Doing so will result in a referral to administration.

___ I understand that classroom work is not private. Switching desktops, closing tabs, or closing applications when the instructor approaches is not permitted.

___ Classroom technology is for school work, with priority to this class. If I finish my assigned work for the day, I am expected to take on the next challenge — not find something to pass time. I may work on other school assignments with permission. Video games, social media, and recreational videos are never allowed.

___ I will remain enrolled in the class Google Classroom for the entire semester. Removing myself bypasses district security monitoring and will result in a referral to administration.

---

## Part 5 — Grading & Consequences

___ I begin each semester with **50 points** in the Safety, 5S & Participation category.

___ **10 points are deducted per incident**, including: failure to wear required PPE, leaving tools or materials out, failure to participate in 5S cleanup, or any other unsafe behavior.

___ Serious or repeated violations — including any violation of the Electronics & Device Use Policy — will be referred to administration, regardless of remaining points.
