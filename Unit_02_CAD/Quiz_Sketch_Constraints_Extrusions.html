Practical Task · Unit 2 CAD

Model a Stepped Block

Apply sketch constraints and extrusion types to build a two-step solid body. Use only constraints to control shape — no Smart Dimensions. Deliverable: screenshot of the finished part with the Feature Tree fully expanded.

Step 1 — Base (Boss Extrusion)

  • Sketch a rectangle on the Front Plane. Apply Horizontal and Vertical constraints to all four edges. Apply a Coincident constraint between one corner and the sketch origin.
  • Add a Smart Dimension: 80 mm wide × 50 mm tall.
  • Boss Extrude the rectangle 25 mm Blind.

Step 2 — Step Feature (Boss Extrusion)

  • On the top face of the base, sketch a smaller rectangle spanning the full 50 mm depth (use Coincident constraints to lock it to both front and rear edges of the top face).
  • Apply an Equal constraint between the two 50 mm edges to confirm they match.
  • Add a Smart Dimension: 40 mm wide. Boss Extrude 15 mm Blind.

Step 3 — Through-Hole (Cut Extrusion)

  • On the top face of the step, sketch a circle. Apply a Coincident constraint between the circle center and the midpoint of the top face.
  • Add a Smart Dimension: diameter 12 mm. Cut Extrude Through All.
