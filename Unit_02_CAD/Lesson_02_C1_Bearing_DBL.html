Press-fit pocket

  • Pocket diameter = 22.0 mm (exact match)
  • Bearing must be pressed in — won't come out by hand
  • Relies on your printer's tolerance being tight
  • Good for permanent installation

Slip-fit pocket

  • Pocket diameter = 22.1–22.2 mm
  • Bearing drops in and can be removed
  • More forgiving of printer variation
  • May need a drop of adhesive to hold

You'll decide which fit to use in Lesson 2.5. For now, model the bearing at exactly 22 mm OD.

Lab: Measure Before You Trust

Get a real 608 bearing from the supply bin. Measure it with calipers. Record your results.

Dimension Nominal (McMaster spec) My measurement Match?
Outer Diameter (OD) 22.0 mm
Inner Diameter (ID) 8.0 mm
Width 7.0 mm
If your measured OD differs from 22.0 mm — use your measured value as the reference for C2 pocket sizing, not the nominal spec. Real parts have tolerances; your caliper tells you the actual size in your hands.
