# Unit 2 CAD — Video Script Template

**Format:** Screencast of Fusion 360 with voiceover  
**Length target:** 8–15 min per component (Level 1 walkthrough only)  
**Level 2/3:** Tagged at end of video as text cards, or moved to the activity guide  
**Student instruction:** Watch the whole video once before opening Fusion 360, then go back and work through it step by step.

---

## INTRO  *(every video — ~45 sec)*

> "Hey, what's up. This is Mr. B — Applied Engineering and Robotics at Vicksburg High School.
>
> In this video we're modeling the **[COMPONENT NAME]**. This is Component [#] in our CAD unit, and it mounts [brief one-phrase location — e.g., "to the front of the L.E.O. chassis" / "under the electronics board"].
>
> One rule before you open Fusion: **watch the whole video first.** Don't try to keep up on the first pass — just watch, understand the build, and then go back to the beginning and work alongside me.
>
> This is the Level 1 walkthrough. Level 1 means you replicate my design exactly — same dimensions, same features. [If Level 2/3 are added at end:] Stay for the end of the video — I'll give you the Level 2 and 3 challenges after the main walkthrough.
>
> Alright, let's get into it."

---

## SETUP BLOCK  *(every video — ~30 sec)*

> "First thing before any sketching — file setup. Create a new Fusion 360 file and name it:
>
> **[YourLastName]_[ComponentName]_v1-Base**
>
> Capital B on Base. Save it to your class folder before you sketch anything — not after. Losing work because you forgot to save is a bad reason to stay after class."

---

## BODY — LEVEL 1 WALKTHROUGH

*[Component-specific. See `component-checklist.md` for tools and spec for each component.]*

### Narration pattern for each step:
1. **State what you're doing** — "I'm going to sketch the base profile on the XZ plane."
2. **Do it** — [perform the action on screen]
3. **State why** — "I'm using a rectangle here because the motor housing is rectangular — starting with the right shape saves time."
4. **Flag constraints** — "Before I extrude, I need this sketch fully constrained. Watch for the lines to turn black."
5. **Say keyboard shortcuts aloud** — "I'm pressing S to open the sketch palette."

---

## OUTRO  *(every video — ~60 sec)*

> "Alright — that's the complete [COMPONENT NAME]. Here's what your finished model should look like. [pause 2–3 sec on finished isometric view]
>
> Now go back to the beginning. Start the video over and build it yourself, step by step. Pause whenever you need to — that's what the pause button is for.
>
> When you're done, double-check your file name: **[YourLastName]_[ComponentName]_v1-Base**. If you made any changes during the build, save a second version with a descriptive suffix — something like v2-FitFix or v2-WiderSlot.
>
> Then open the **Activity 2.[#] guide** in the class hub. You've got a five-question learning check and your screenshot submission. Get those done before you submit your deliverable in the Portfolio.
>
> [Sign-off — choose one or rotate:]
> - 'Get to work.'
> - 'See you in the next one.'
> - 'Now go build something.'"

## LEVEL 2 TAG  *(end of video — ~30 sec, or move to activity guide)*

> "If you're working on Level 2: [specific modification prompt — see component-checklist.md for each component]
>
> Make at least one meaningful design change — improve fit, reduce material, or add a functional feature. Save your modified version as **v2-[DescriptiveName]** — something like v2-LightWeight or v2-WideBolt. Document what you changed and why in your activity guide."

---

## LEVEL 3 TAG  *(end of video — ~30 sec, or move to activity guide)*

> "If you're working on Level 3: start from scratch. Same functional requirements, completely different design. The constraints are in the activity guide — meet those, and everything else is up to you. Save it as **v1-Custom**."

---

## RECORDING CHECKLIST

Before hitting record, confirm:
- [ ] Fusion 360 open with a fresh file, UI panels at default size
- [ ] Screen resolution readable at 1080p (test with a student device if possible)
- [ ] Zoom in to the active tool/cursor before using it — students need to see where you're clicking
- [ ] Mic levels tested — voiceover should be audible over any fan noise
- [ ] Timeline cleared — close unrelated tabs and notifications

During recording:
- [ ] Say every keyboard shortcut aloud the first time you use it
- [ ] Pause 1–2 sec after placing a dimension so students can read the value
- [ ] Call out "fully constrained" every time the sketch lines go black
- [ ] Mention the file-save step at Setup and again at Outro
