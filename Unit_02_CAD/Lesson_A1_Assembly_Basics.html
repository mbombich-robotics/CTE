Assembly Concepts

Assembly file vs. part file

  • A part design file holds one component
  • An assembly design file holds many components and defines how they relate
  • The first component you insert becomes the anchor — everything else positions relative to it
  • Saving the assembly saves any components you edited during that session

Constraint types used

  • Flush — two flat faces share a plane (with or without offset)
  • Concentric — two cylindrical surfaces share an axis
  • Angular — aligns flat faces rotationally (used for hex flats)
  • Edge-to-Edge — two circular edges share axis AND plane — seats a screw in one step

The edge-to-edge trick

In the Constraint tool: select the circular edge at the floor of the counterbore (where the flat bottom meets the cylindrical wall), then select the circular edge on the underside of the screw head (where the flat face meets the shank). Two circles, same axis, same plane — one constraint does the work of two.
