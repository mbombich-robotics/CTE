Unit 1: Engineering Design Process

Master the Engineering Design Process through a 7-lesson sequence that mirrors how engineers actually work. You'll experience designing without a framework, learn the framework, and then apply it to a full design cycle — defining the problem, interviewing the client, sketching concepts, and choosing the best one before any modeling begins. PLTW POE 0.1 content integrated throughout.
