Shop Safety — Before Anything Else

Top 5 Rules

  • No horseplay — ever
  • Always wear required PPE (safety glasses, closed-toe shoes)
  • No loose clothing or dangling jewelry near machines
  • Don't touch any machine you haven't been certified on
  • If something looks wrong — stop and ask

We will cover full OSHA/LOTO/SDS and tool certifications in Unit 3 (October). For now: these 5 rules apply every day, starting today.

Career Connection PLTW

Albert Manero | Mechanical Engineer

As a PLTW student, Albert was inspired by 3D printing in his engineering class.

He noticed that prosthetic limbs for children cost tens of thousands of dollars — far out of reach for most families.

He started Limbitless Solutions, which 3D prints free bionic arms for children in need.

The Pattern

  • Noticed a problem in his community
  • Had skills from an engineering class
  • Built a solution that helped real people

Engineers notice problems and do something about them.
