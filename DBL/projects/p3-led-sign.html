Design & Build Lab · Project 3

LED Sign &
Wall Art

Design and fabricate an illuminated sign or piece of wall art using CNC machining and addressable LED lighting. Your artwork is engraved or cut into your chosen material — then lit from behind or the edge with 12V LED strips that make the design glow. Every piece gets mounted and displayed.

4–5 Weeks CNC Fabrication LED Electronics CAD + Design 3 Deliverables Individual

Size Constraint — Required

Your finished piece — sign, frame, stand, and all electronics — must fit inside a 12" × 12" × 6" cube. Design your sign dimensions and mounting method with this constraint in mind from the start.

Choose Your Fabrication Path

🔵
Laser Engraving
xTool laser → xTool Creative Space or Lightburn

Engrave your design into clear acrylic. LEDs light the edge of the sheet — the etched lines scatter the light and glow. Cut the acrylic silhouette to match your design shape for maximum effect.

Clear acrylic Edge-lit SVG / DXF Most visual impact
🟡
CNC Router
OMIO X8 → Fusion 360 CAM toolpath

Mill your design into wood, MDF, or acrylic with a V-bit or ball-nose. Shallow relief carves catch light and shadow. Combine with LED backlighting or strip lighting around the perimeter.

Wood / MDF / acrylic Relief carving F360 CAM Paintable
🟠
CNC Plasma
Crossfire Pro → F360 CAM toolpath

Cut your design through sheet metal. Backlight with LED strips and the cutouts glow through the metal — a bold, industrial look. Design must account for "kerf" width and connected geometry.

Steel / aluminum Backlit cutouts F360 CAM Industrial look

Every Design Must Include

🎨
Original Artwork

Your design. A logo, character, pattern, word, or scene — drawn or sourced and then adapted for your chosen fabrication method.

💡
LED Integration

12V addressable LED strips wired and positioned to illuminate the finished piece. The lighting method must match your material choice.

🔩
Mounting / Stand

A method to display the finished piece — a stand, wall bracket, or frame. This is a designed component, not an afterthought. Fits within the size constraint.

🔁
Documented Iteration

At least one design change made after your first test — adjusted design, lighting position, mounting method, or assembly fit — documented in your iteration log.

⚠️ LED control method TBD: The addressable LED control approach — simple remote controller vs. programmed microcontroller — will be confirmed before D_P3B. Check with Mr. Bombich before finalizing your electronics plan.

Deliverables

D_P3A
Week 1–2
Planning
Design Brief & Concept Plan

Define your project before you touch any machine. Sketch your ideas, select your fabrication path, and plan how the sign, LEDs, and mount come together.

  • Problem statement and design statement (who is this for, what does it need to do)
  • At least 2 concept sketches of your artwork and overall form
  • Fabrication path selected: laser, router, or plasma — with written justification
  • Mounting / stand method sketched with dimensions
  • Decision matrix confirming your chosen concept
  • Submitted to portfolio before starting any CAD or machine work
D_P3B
Week 2–3
Digital Files
Fabrication Files & Cut Preview

Your complete set of machine-ready files, reviewed and ready to run. This deliverable is due before your first machine time.

  • Final design file: F360 model with CAM toolpath (router/plasma) or SVG/DXF prepared in xTool Creative Space (laser)
  • Screenshot: design preview showing cut/engrave paths
  • Screenshot: mounting/stand design in F360 or as a separate sketch
  • Material and stock dimensions documented
  • LED strip placement plan — diagram showing where strips go relative to the sign
  • Tinkercad Circuits screenshot — ATtiny85 + LED strip wired correctly (correct pins, resistors, capacitors, and USB power)
D_P3C
Week 4–5
Finished Product
Assembled & Illuminated Sign

Your finished, mounted, and lit sign — ready for the wall. Both a dark-room photo (lights on) and a daylight photo (lights off) required so the fabrication quality is visible.

  • Photo: sign fully lit in a darkened space — shows the LED effect
  • Photo: sign in normal light — shows fabrication quality and mounting
  • Photo: detail shot of the mounting / stand assembly
  • Finished piece fits within the 12" × 12" × 6" size constraint
  • Iteration log: at least one entry documenting a design or assembly change and its result

Grading Levels

C
Level 1 — Complete

A finished, illuminated sign that fits the size constraint. Artwork is clearly readable, LEDs are functional, and a basic mounting method is included. All three deliverables submitted on time with an iteration log entry.

B
Level 2 — Refined

Complete Level 1, plus: design incorporates multiple elements or layers (e.g., silhouette + interior engraving, or sign + decorative frame). Lighting placement is intentional and enhances the design. Iteration log shows a meaningful change with before/after evidence.

A
Level 3 — Exceptional

Complete Level 2, plus: the sign, electronics, and mounting form a cohesive, professional-quality piece. May include addressable LED color control, multi-material construction, or an original mounting system designed in F360. Documented with the rigor of a professional portfolio piece.
