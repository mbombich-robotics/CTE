Concept Sketching

Sketching is an important skill for biomedical scientists, engineers, and designers. With a concept sketch, you can quickly and clearly communicate design intent and details. Representing existing objects and new ideas with sketches can make the design process more effective and efficient.

TermDefinition
concept sketchA graphical representation of an idea that can quickly communicate design intent and details.
design processA systematic problem-solving strategy, with criteria and constraints, used to develop many possible solutions to solve a problem or satisfy human needs and wants and to winnow (narrow) down the possible solutions to one final choice.

Concept Sketching Tips 

Concept sketches don’t need to be perfect. While it is important that they accurately communicate your thinking, they don’t need to show precise measurements and detailed annotations. This video demonstrates best practices for developing concept sketches.
